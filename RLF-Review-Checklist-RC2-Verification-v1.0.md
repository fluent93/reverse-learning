## Part II: TechTrends Peer Reviewer Perspective (RC2 Evaluation)

### 1. Internal Consistency with the Framework

**Score: 93/100 (up from 90)**
**Verdict: Excellent — all prior alignment gaps closed**

The RC2 maintains all the consistency gains achieved in the RC (Stage 1 reframing, Stage 2/4 boundary, epistemic ownership definition) and adds two structural improvements that further strengthen alignment:

1. **Theoretical Anchors** make the connection between each stage and the framework's theoretical foundations explicit, rather than relying on the reader to infer them.
2. **The Process Note** acknowledges the recursive nature of the framework, which aligns with the One-Pager's treatment of the stages as a flexible process rather than a rigid pipeline.

**Minor observation:** The section formerly titled "What This Checklist Is—and Is Not" has been split into two separate sections: "Important Boundary Note" (ML terminology disambiguation) and a retained theoretical positioning paragraph within that note. This separation is cleaner than combining the two concerns. However, the positioning text now sits inside the "Important Boundary Note" section, which may cause readers to conflate the ML disambiguation purpose with the pedagogical positioning purpose.

**Recommendation for final:** Consider renaming "Important Boundary Note" to something more encompassing (e.g., "Scope and Positioning") or splitting it into two clearly separate subsections within the same block: one for terminological disambiguation and one for pedagogical positioning relative to PBL/IBL/experiential learning. Alternatively, the current structure works well enough — this is an editorial nuance, not a structural flaw.

### 2. Theoretical Grounding

**Score: 90/100 (up from 68)**
**Verdict: Transformed — the largest single improvement across all dimensions**

This dimension was the weakest element in the RC (68/100) and has now become a strength. Each Part includes a "Theoretical Anchor" subsection with a concise statement linking the stage to established learning theory:

| Part | Theoretical Anchor | Quality Assessment |
|---|---|---|

**Strengths:**
- The quantified thresholds ("at least one," "at least two") transform vague self-reports into observable, verifiable actions.

**Remaining gaps:**
1. **Part 1 items remain largely unchanged** from the RC. Items like "I labeled the AI output as provisional, not final" are already behaviorally clear, so this is not a concern. 2. **Part 7 items are intentionally self-report** ("I can explain...", "I can justify...", "I can respond..."). This is appropriate because Part 7 is about demonstrating capability, which is validated through the Evidence Checkpoint (answering ownership questions) and optional oral defense. The self-report format here is a feature, not a bug.
3. **No peer review mechanism** is scaffolded within the checklist itself. This was noted in the RC review and remains unaddressed. However, the Instructor/Facilitator Notes include "a peer review guide" as a use case, which sufficiently signals the possibility.

### RC2의 핵심 개선 사항

1. **이론적 앵커(Theoretical Anchors)** — 가장 큰 단일 개선(68점→90점). 7개 단계 모두에 학습이론과의 연결을 명시하는 "Theoretical Anchor" 하위 섹션이 추가되었다. 구성주의, 비판적 사고, 정보 리터러시, 메타인지, 상황학습, 생성적 학습, 자기 설명 등 확립된 학습과학 전통에 각 단계를 연결한다.

2. **행동 구체성** — 체크리스트 항목에 정량화된 행동 기준이 추가되었다. "주장을 식별했다"가 "최소 2개의 구체적 주장을 표시했다"로, "경험에서 예시를 추가했다"가 "최소 1개의 예시를 추가했다"로 변경되는 등 관찰 가능하고 검증 가능한 행동으로 전환했다.

3. **프로세스 노트** — "7개 단계는 명확성을 위해 순차적으로 제시되지만, 실제 학습은 종종 재귀적"이라는 점을 명시하여 비선형적 학습 현실을 인정했다.

4. **경계 노트** — "Reverse Learning"이 기계학습의 역학습/역강화학습과 무관함을 명확히 구분하여 학제간 혼동을 방지했다.

5. **빠른 시작 버전 강화** — 7개 질문에 "무엇을 산출할 것인가(What to Produce)" 열을 추가하여 구체적 산출물을 지정했다. 10분 안에 자기 완결적으로 사용 가능하다.

6. **한글 요약 업데이트** — 근거 체크포인트, 사용 수준, 빠른 시작 버전을 반영하여 이중 언어 콘텐츠의 동기화가 크게 개선되었다.
